1. Overview

You can delete your Lain Dain account at any time, directly from the app. Deleting your account is permanent and cannot be undone, so please read the requirements below before you start.

2. Before You Delete

To delete your account, you must first:

  • Clear all your pending ledgers. Every ledger where you owe money or are owed money must be settled and cleared. Accounts with pending ledgers cannot be deleted.
  • Make sure you have no unsettled balances in any group or individual ledger.
  • Save any records, notes, or attachments you want to keep, as they will not be recoverable after deletion.

3. How to Delete Your Account

Once all your pending ledgers are cleared:

  1. Open the Lain Dain app and sign in.
  2. Go to Account Settings.
  3. Tap the Delete Account option.
  4. Confirm when prompted.

4. What Happens Next

  • Your account and profile information are deleted.
  • You will be signed out and will no longer be able to access Lain Dain with this account.
  • Certain records may be retained where required for legal, security, fraud-prevention, or audit purposes, as described in our Privacy Policy.

5. Can't Delete Your Account?

If the Delete Account option shows an error, check that all your ledgers are fully cleared. If everything is settled and you still can't delete your account, contact support below.
